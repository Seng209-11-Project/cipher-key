Privacy Policy for CipherKey

Last updated: November 24, 2025

1. Data Collection CipherKey is designed with privacy as its top priority. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, passwords, or data entered into this application.

2. Data Storage (Local Only) All data entered into this application (including usernames, passwords, and notes) is stored locally on your device using the device's native secure storage (Shared Preferences / Encrypted Storage).

We do not have a backend server.

We cannot access your passwords.

If you uninstall the application or clear your device data, your stored passwords will be deleted permanently because we do not have a backup copy.

3. Third-Party Services This application does not use any third-party analytics, tracking services, or advertising networks. No data is shared with third parties.

4. Security We utilize standard device security features to protect the data stored locally on your device. However, please note that no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We encourage users to keep their device software updated and use a secure device lock screen.

5. Contact Us If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at cipherkeycontact@gmail.com.
